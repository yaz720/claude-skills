## 英文版模板

结构完全对应，标题依次为：

1. Case Summary
2. Red Flag Screening
3. Assumptions and Information Gaps
4. Current Evidence and How It Evolved
5. The Consulting Panel
6. Round 1: Independent Positions
   - Adversarial Seat: Register of Objections (with strength grading)
7. Round 2: Cross-Examination
8. Round 3: Convergence and Mapping Disagreement
   - Settlement of Objections (status: stands / qualified / refuted — refuted ones must be retained)
   - Same Data, Opposite Readings *(only if applicable)*
8b. Treatment Options Compared *(conditional section — omit when consensus is "no intervention")*
9. Position Comparison Table
10. Consensus (Actionable)
11. Unresolved Disagreements
12. Monitoring and Return Precautions
13. Questions for Your Doctor
14. Sources
+ Disclaimer

### 英文版术语处理

中医概念用**拼音 + 汉字**，不做意译：

| 中文 | 英文版写法 |
|---|---|
| 气虚 | qi deficiency (气虚, qì xū) |
| 血瘀 | blood stasis (血瘀, xuè yū) |
| 湿热下注 | damp-heat pouring downward (湿热下注, shī rè xià zhù) |
| 补阳还五汤 | Buyang Huanwu Decoction (补阳还五汤) |
| 辨证论治 | pattern differentiation and treatment (辨证论治, biàn zhèng lùn zhì) |

方剂名用拼音全称 + 汉字，不译成 "Tonify Yang and Restore Five Decoction" 这类容易误解的直译。

若英文版可能给英语国家医生阅读，检查结果单位注意换算（如血糖 mmol/L ↔ mg/dL），并同时给出两种单位。

## 中文版术语处理

专业名词首次出现时括注英文原词：

> 浅静脉反流（superficial venous reflux）· 射频消融（radiofrequency ablation, RFA）· 需治疗人数（number needed to treat, NNT）· 人类白细胞抗原 B27（HLA-B27）

缩写首次出现时给全称，后续可直接用缩写。
